# Findings (2023 is the latest full year in the data)

- **Volume:** 2.45 billion boardings in 2023, 6.71 million per day (+5.1% vs. 2022).
- **Pandemic and recovery:** daily boardings fell to 11% of the early-2020 level in 2020-04; they first returned to that level in 2022-05.
- **Mode mix:** Bus 54%, Rail 43%, Sea 3% of boardings in 2023.
- **Peak hour:** weekday boardings peak at 18:00; weekends carry 72% of a weekday's volume.
- **Transfers and fares:** 9% of boardings are transfers; discounted cards 47%, free cards 13%.
- **Busiest station:** Yenikapi (M1), 21.8 million boardings.
- **Busiest line:** 34 (AVCILAR-ZINCIRLIKUYU), 234.9 million boardings.
- **Holidays:** the deepest drop is on Feast of Sacrifice, -16% vs. comparable days.

Charts are in `docs/figures/`. Every number above is recomputed by `python analysis/report.py`.
